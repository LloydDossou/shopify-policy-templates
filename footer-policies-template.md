## Terms & Conditions

### Payment

Payment is charged immediately upon placing an order through secure payment solutions.  
We accept payment via Vipps, credit/debit card, and Klarna.

All prices are listed in Norwegian Kroner (NOK) and include applicable VAT (MVA), unless otherwise stated.

---

### Shipping & Delivery

We offer free shipping on all orders within Norway.

Orders are processed within **24–72 hours** after placement.  
Estimated delivery time is **3–5 business days**, depending on your location.

Once your order has been shipped, you will receive tracking information via SMS or email.

---

### Right of Withdrawal & Returns

You have a **14-day right of withdrawal** from the day you receive your order, in accordance with the Norwegian Right of Withdrawal Act (Angrerettloven).

To exercise your right of withdrawal:

- The item must be unused  
- The item must be unopened  
- The item must be returned in its original packaging  

Once the returned item has been received and inspected, a full refund will be issued.

Return shipping costs are covered by the customer.  
Items that are opened, used, or damaged are not eligible for a full refund.

---

### Complaints / Defective Items

If you receive a product that is defective or damaged upon arrival, you must contact us **within 7 days** of receipt and provide documentation (photo or video).

Once the issue has been reviewed and approved, we will offer a replacement product at no additional cost.

The following are **not** considered defects and are not covered under complaint rights:

- Improper use  
- Normal wear and tear  
- Minor cosmetic imperfections
